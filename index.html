Services

LAMINATE FLOORING

Laminate flooring is a popular choice for homeowners due to its durability, affordability, and wide range of designs available at morning floor. It consists of several layers bonded together to create a stable and versatile floor covering.

CUSHION VINYL

Cushion vinyl, also known as luxury vinyl flooring, is a type of flooring that provides a softer and more cushioned feel underfoot compared to traditional vinyl flooring. It is made of multiple layers, including a backing layer, a vinyl layer with a wooden printed design, and a clear wear layer that protects against scratches and stains

ENGINEERED FLOORING

Engineered flooring is a type of flooring that is made up of multiple layers of wood or wood composite veneers. It consists of a thin layer of real hardwood on top, known as the wear layer, and multiple layers of plywood or high-density fiberboard (HDF) underneath, known as the core layers

SPC FLOORING

SPC flooring stands for Stone Plastic Composite flooring. It is a type of rigid core flooring that is made from a combination of limestone and PVC resin. SPC flooring is becoming increasingly popular due to its durability, waterproof features, and realistic wood or stone look.

BAMBOO FLOORING

Bamboo flooring is a type of flooring made from the bamboo plant. It is an eco-friendly and sustainable flooring option, as bamboo is a rapidly renewable resource. Bamboo flooring is known for its durability and strength, making it a popular choice for high-traffic areas.

CARPET TILES

Carpet tiles are small, individual sections of carpet that can be installed to create a complete carpeted floor. They are typically square in shape, ranging in size from 18 inches to 36 inches and are available in a variety of colors, patterns, and textures..

INDUSTRIAL FLOORING

Industrial flooring refers to flooring materials and systems that are specifically designed to withstand heavy use, harsh environments, and demanding conditions typically found in industrial facilities. These types of flooring are highly durable, resistant to chemicals, slip-resistant, and able to withstand heavy machinery and equipment.

COMPOSITE DECKING

Composite decking is a type of decking material made from a combination of wood fibers and plastic. It is an alternative to traditional wood decking and offers several advantages such as durability, low maintenance, and resistance to rot and insect damage.

TURF GLASS

Turf grass refers to a type of grass that is specifically cultivated and maintained for use as a ground cover in lawns, parks, sports fields, and other recreational areas. It is often chosen for its uniform, thick, and lush appearance, as well as its ability to withstand heavy foot traffic.

VINYL PLANK FLOORING

Vinyl plank flooring is a type of synthetic flooring that mimics the appearance of hardwood flooring. It consists of individual planks made from layers of pvc materials that are compressed together to form a durable and water-resistant surface.
